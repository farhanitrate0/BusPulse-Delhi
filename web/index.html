Why we differ from Google
Google Maps is an estimate too. For buses it starts from the published timetable and adjusts it with live data or traffic predictions where it has them; in Delhi at 6 pm its times barely moved. So a gap from Google is not an error: the real buses decide. Every BusPulse number on this page is scored against real bus trips, not against Google.
