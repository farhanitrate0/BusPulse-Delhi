# Who rides Delhi's buses: user-research brief

Desk research, Nov 2026. "Dated" = data before 2019. Ranges shown where sources disagree.

## Key numbers

- **~41 lakh bus trips/day** (DTC + cluster): 41.4 lakh in 2022-23 ([Economic Survey of Delhi 2023-24](https://delhiplanning.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/Planning/chapter_12.pdf)); ~40.8 lakh in 2024-25 ([Survey 2025-26 via NewsBytes, 2026](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/auto/economic-survey-2025-26-finds-delhi-bus-ridership-down-nearly-20/story)); down from 51 lakh in 2019-20.
- **Women: 30–42% of bus trips.** 30% = our calculation from Survey 2022-23 data; 33% (2022-23) = government estimate ([I4I](https://www.ideasforindia.in/topics/social-identity/fighting-gender-inequality-through-mobility-assessing-delhi-s-pink-ticket-scheme.html)); 42% (2023) = [WRI India, 2025](https://theprint.in/india/governance/aaps-free-bus-ride-scheme-helped-delhi-women-save-up-to-8-get-access-to-better-jobs-wri-india-paper/2536921).
- **Fares: ₹5–15 non-AC, ₹10–25 AC** ([Delhi Tourism, GNCTD](https://delhitourism.gov.in/transport/city_bus.html); undated page, checked 2026).
- **87% of women wait >10 min at the stop, 13% >30 min; only 21% say buses arrive on time** (n=510, [Greenpeace India, 2024](https://www.greenpeace.org/static/planet4-india-stateless/2024/10/c293ab29-riding-the-justice-route-1-compressed.pdf)).
- **>50% of bus stops have scheduled average waits >15 min** (timetable analysis, [CSE, 2025](https://cseindia.org/content/downloadreports/12612)).
- **Load factor ~81%** (DTC 81.67%, cluster 81%, 2022-23; Survey 2023-24).
- **74% of Delhi women have a mobile phone they use themselves** ([NFHS-5, 2019-21](https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/FR374/FR374_Delhi.pdf)).

- **Metro, for comparison:** ~46 lakh daily passenger journeys, 2022-23 (Survey 2023-24; counts interchanges).
- **Free travel for women:** 45.4 crore free trips in 2022-23 (Survey 2023-24); ~100 crore pink tickets by 2024 ([Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/india/en/press/16992/women-reclaim-public-spaces-with-zero-fare-pink-tickets-yet-safety-and-accessibility-gaps-remain-says-greenpeace-report/)). From 1 Oct 2026 free rides need a Pink Saheli card, issued only to Delhi residents; ~19 lakh issued ([ThePrint, Oct 2026](https://theprint.in/feature/delhis-free-bus-ride-scheme-address-proof-migrant-women/3060875/)).

## 2. Fares and spend

- Non-AC: ₹5 (≤4 km), ₹10 (4–10 km), ₹15 (>10 km). AC: ₹10 (≤4 km), ₹15 (4–8), ₹20 (8–12), ₹25 (>12) (Delhi Tourism).
- Passes: daily ₹40 non-AC / ₹50 AC; monthly all-route ₹800 / ₹1,000; student ₹100 ([DTC](https://dtc.delhi.gov.in/dtc/get-dtc-pass), checked 2026).
- Typical daily spend: not found in a survey. Our arithmetic: ~₹20 round trip non-AC, ₹30–50 AC.
- Free travel saved women ₹500–2,300/month; transport had taken ~8% of income in economically weaker households (WRI 2025, 2023 survey).

## 3. Trip profile

- **Trip length:** women's average bus trip 7.92 → 10.52 km after free travel (WRI, 2023). All-mode average 10.9 km in 2018 (CSE 2025 citing MPD-2041; dated).
- **Duration:** 71% of bus users travel <30 min/day incl. waiting; 13% >1 hour ([Greenpeace 2021](https://www.greenpeace.org/static/planet4-india-stateless/2021/12/6e89d8b1-bustling_through_the_city.pdf)).
- **Headways:** in the morning peak only ~25% of DTC and 41% of cluster routes ran every ≤15 min ([CSE, 2017](https://cseindia.org/content/downloadreports/8114); dated).
- **Peak bus speed:** not found. All traffic slows 41% (morning) and 56% (evening) below free-flow on weekdays (CSE 2025, Sept 2024 data).

## 4. Who rides

- **Income:** ~50% of women bus users surveyed have household income <₹30k/month; most common personal income ≤₹10k (Greenpeace 2024).
- **No car:** ~95% of WRI's 2023 sample lacked a private vehicle; 10% of Greenpeace 2021 respondents owned a car.
- **Purpose:** ~90% of women ride for work, education or caregiving (WRI 2025).
- **Modal split:** bus + metro 24% of trips, private vehicles 29% (2018; dated, CSE 2025 citing MPD-2041). Current bus-only share: not found.
- **First/last mile:** ~75% walk to the stop, 20% e-rickshaw (Greenpeace 2021); 76% of women walk >10 min to a stop (Greenpeace 2024).

## 5. Pain points

- **Reliability:** 21% say buses arrive on time; 7% say buses halt long enough to board (Greenpeace 2024). 76% of terminal users cite poor frequency ([Safetipin for DTC, 2023](https://safetipin.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Assessment-of-safety-accessibility-of-Delhi-bus-terminals-safetipin-2023.pdf)).
- **Information:** 56% of women say stops lack key route/schedule info (Greenpeace 2024).
- **Crowding:** ~81% load factor; women ask for occupancy limits (Greenpeace 2024).
- **Share of riders who don't know when the bus will come:** not found.

## 6. Digital access and language

- 98% of urban Delhi households have a mobile phone; 74% of women have one they use themselves, 82% of whom can read SMS (NFHS-5, 2019-21).
- Smartphone penetration among bus riders: not found. Greenpeace (2024) notes many homemakers and casual labourers have no phone.
- Hindi: 80.9% mother tongue in Delhi, Census 2001 ([Univ. Laval compilation](https://axl.cefan.ulaval.ca/EtatsNsouverains/inde-delhi.htm); dated). 2011 Delhi figure: not found.
- Live GPS refreshes every 10 s on Delhi's open feed; One Delhi tracks 7,300+ buses ([Swarajya, 2026](https://swarajyamag.com/reports/delhi-has-the-buses-the-data-and-the-apps-why-cant-you-still-find-your-bus)).

## What this means for BusPulse

- **Door-to-door times with real waits:** most riders wait >10 min and walk >10 min; times that ignore both understate the trip.
- **"On 9 of 10 days" ranges:** with only 21% saying buses are on time, a reliable range is more honest than one ETA.
- **Crowding layer:** buses average ~81% full; showing crowding helps pick which bus to board.
- **Hindi, big-number UI:** riders are mostly Hindi speakers and low-income, and a quarter of women lack a phone of their own; large numbers and little text suit low-end and shared phones.
- **Live buses:** live GPS is already public, and riders report long waits and poor stop information; live positions answer "when will my bus come?"
